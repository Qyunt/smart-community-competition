# 4.2 m competition texture package

`materials/textures/` contains the source-derived person, car, signal and
deterministically typeset plate PNGs. `materials/scripts/` registers their
Gazebo Ogre names. `asset_manifest.json` records source and plate semantics.

The blue and green parking targets use a cutout of the user-provided sedan.
The yellow target uses a generated transparent large-passenger rear image so
the plate colour matches its vehicle class. The 18 people were separated from
the page 5 training montage. Signal state textures are copies of the user's
three supplied photos. The four plate types are blue small car, yellow large
vehicle, green small pure-electric vehicle, and white police vehicle. These
numbers are illustrative rendered assets, not issued registration numbers.
